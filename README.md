VICE FRAME is an offline cover studio that turns any photo and a caption into GTA 6-style cover art: a multi-panel collage with a Vice City neon-sunset grade, stacked gradient lettering and an original VI badge, exported as a shareable PNG. This edition adds a bundled Cast & Scenes library — eleven original illustrated plates drawn in a flat neon-poster style: six characters (the two leads, a white-suited kingpin, a street racer, a lifeguard and a biker, each on their own scenic backdrop) and five neon-coast scenes (skyline, marina, street, beach and swamp) — plus a one-click Showcase mode that casts those plates into a finished blockbuster cover, ready to restyle with the grade presets or remix with your own photos. It runs entirely in the browser — upload or drop a photo (or use a bundled sample or plate), arrange it across the panels, pick a layout and grade, and export at portrait, square or wide sizes. No uploads, no network, no accounts: your photos never leave your device.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
